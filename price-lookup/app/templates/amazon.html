Fast (recommended): install the free Amazon Order History Reporter (azad) browser extension. Open it while logged into amazon.com.au, pick a date range, and export the Items report as CSV. Works in your browser so 2FA just works — no waiting.

Official (slow): Request Your Data → "Your Orders" → confirm by email → download the archive (can take days to weeks) → unzip → Retail.OrderHistory.1.csv.
